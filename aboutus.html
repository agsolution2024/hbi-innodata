About Us

Emerging from the core group of Exquisite Traders, Inc., an industrial solutions company established in the mid 80s,
BHI-Innodata was incorporated to address the increasing demands of both existing and new clients, for business and IT solutions. Comprised mainly of seasoned solution architects plus certified technology and business process consultants, BHI-Innodata has been providing end-to-end best practice consulting services for business growth and IT solutions spanning across various industries. The company prides itself on keeping a healthy working partnership with each client thus, resulting in a vast majority having on-going engagements for more than a decade.
