About Us

Innodata traces its roots to Exquisite Traders Inc, an industrial solutions company that began operations in the mid 80s. As the business landscape evolved and technology became central to organizational success, Innodata was incorporated to meet the increasing demands of both long standing and emerging clients. Our purpose has always been clear: to help businesses navigate growth, modernization, and operational challenges through dependable expertise, sound systems, and tailored solutions that create lasting impact.

Over the years, our team has grown into a strong group of seasoned solution architects, certified technology specialists, and business process consultants. Together, we deliver end to end services that include best practice consulting, systems development, infrastructure solutions, and implementation support. Our approach centers on understanding how each client operates, identifying the gaps that slow them down, and providing the right tools and strategies to strengthen their performance. This commitment to quality work and genuine collaboration has allowed us to support organizations across multiple industries, many of whom have trusted us for decades.

Innodata continues to take pride in building long term partnerships anchored on integrity, transparency, and consistent results. We believe that true value comes not only from delivering solutions but also from helping clients adapt, grow, and thrive in a competitive digital world. As we move forward, our vision remains the same: to be a trusted partner for businesses looking to improve their systems, elevate their capabilities, and unlock smarter, more sustainable ways of working.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to empower businesses with efficient, technology driven solutions that strengthen operations while reducing unnecessary waste. By streamlining processes and moving key workflows online, we help organizations limit paper consumption, minimize manual tasks, and adopt more sustainable, eco-friendly business practices. We exist to support companies in working smarter, faster, and more responsibly for the long term.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver high quality consulting, systems development, and technology services that consistently meet the evolving needs of our clients. We are committed to building solutions that are practical, scalable, and rooted in best practices. As we grow, we aim to expand our reach beyond local industries and serve clients across international markets, ensuring that our expertise and services meet global standards.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted name in business and technology solutions not only in the Philippines but across the world. We aspire to be recognized internationally for our professionalism, technical excellence, and client centered approach. By continuously improving our capabilities and cultivating strong global partnerships, we aim to serve international clients and establish Innodata as a competitive player in the global consulting and IT solutions space.
